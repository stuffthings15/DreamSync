# DreamSync Privacy Policy

Effective: July 19, 2026

DreamSync is published by Remote. This policy explains how the Android phone and Wear OS apps handle user data.

## Data used by DreamSync

During a night session, DreamSync uses watch motion and heart-rate readings to estimate cue-ready windows and pause cues when waking is detected. The app may also store session history, cue events, morning check-ins, and optional dream notes.

## Local processing

DreamSync processes session data on the user's paired phone and watch. Remote does not operate a DreamSync server and does not receive health readings, session history, check-ins, or dream notes. DreamSync has no account system, ads, analytics, or automatic cloud upload.

## Phone and watch transfer

DreamSync transfers settings, cue requests, status, and session summaries between the user's paired Android phone and Wear OS watch through the Google Play services Wearable Data Layer. This transfer is required for core phone-watch operation.

## Export and deletion

Export creates a local file only after the user chooses a destination. Delete removes DreamSync session history, cue events, check-ins, and received watch logs from the phone. Uninstalling the apps removes their local app data, subject to device settings and backups.

## Permissions

- Activity recognition and body-sensor or heart-rate permissions support cue timing and safety.
- Notifications and foreground services keep an active night session visible and provide a stop control.
- DreamSync does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or payment information.

## Intended use

DreamSync is a wellness tool for adults. It does not measure EEG, diagnose sleep, treat a medical condition, or guarantee deeper sleep or lucid dreams.

# DreamSync Official Brand Messaging

## Slogan

Dreams Redefined.

## Mission

DreamSync's mission is to protect sleep continuity by automatically estimating likely sleep from wearable signals and staying quiet whenever confidence, signal quality, or recovery is uncertain.

## Short public message

Automatic sleep estimation with sleep-first safety.

## Watch compatibility

DreamSync targets Android 8.0+ phones with Google Play services and Wear OS 3+ watches that pass live motion, heart-rate, permission, software, and companion-link checks. Core watches use standard Wear OS signals. Full-sensor watches automatically keep every available approved advanced feature. Eligible Samsung Galaxy Watch and Google Pixel Watch models can use the core profile. First physical validation targets Galaxy Watch Ultra 2024 and 2025. Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Galaxy Fit, and older Samsung Tizen watches are not supported in this release.

## Approved claim language

- Estimates likely sleep and conservative cue-ready windows.
- Designed to protect sleep continuity.
- Stays quiet when required safety checks are uncertain.
- Uses a research-shaped paired pink-noise pattern.
- Data stays on paired devices unless the user exports it.
- Shows estimated awakenings, wake after sleep onset, longest uninterrupted bout, cue-linked arousal rate, and morning disturbance rating.

## Do not claim

- Guaranteed uninterrupted sleep or prevention of waking.
- Proven deeper sleep, sleep-stage detection, or EEG phase locking.
- Improved memory, immunity, hormones, or treatment of a condition.
- Physical validation on a watch model until that model has passed documented hardware tests.

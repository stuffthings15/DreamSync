# DreamSync Play Declarations

## App access

- All functionality is available without an account or sign-in.

## Ads

- No ads.

## Data safety

- Data collected by Remote: No.
- Data shared by Remote: No.
- Health, session, survey, and note data is processed locally on the user's paired devices.
- User-initiated export writes only to the destination selected by the user.
- Phone-watch messages use the Google Play services Wearable Data Layer.
- Users can delete local records in the app.

## Health declaration

- Health feature: Sleep Management.
- Not a medical device.
- No diagnosis, treatment, medical monitoring, or clinical decision support.
- Motion and heart rate are used for sleep-cue timing and safety.

## Target audience

- Adults 18 and older.
- Not designed for children.

## Other declarations

- Government app: No.
- Financial features: None.
- User-generated content shared with others: No.
- Location, camera, microphone, contacts, advertising ID, and payments: Not used.

# DreamSync Privacy Policy

Effective: July 19, 2026

DreamSync is published by Remote. This policy explains how the Android phone and Wear OS apps handle user data.

## Data used by DreamSync

During a night session, DreamSync uses watch motion and heart-rate readings to estimate cue-ready windows. On supported Galaxy Watches, the optional Samsung sensor integration can also use inter-beat intervals, locally derived HRV/RMSSD, skin and ambient temperature trends, short raw-PPG signal-quality windows, and off-wrist status. Raw PPG values are not stored; DreamSync stores only quality counts. Any arousal sign starts a 20-minute quiet period, and the first cue-linked wake sign stops further cues for the night. The app may also store session history, cue events, morning check-ins, and optional dream notes.

## Research basis and limits

DreamSync copies the paired pink-noise burst structure reported in peer-reviewed auditory sleep research. Those laboratory studies used EEG to phase-lock sounds to brain waves. DreamSync does not measure EEG, diagnose sleep stages, or send data to the study authors. Research results about slow oscillations, memory, hormones, or immune cells are not claims about DreamSync's effects.

## Local processing

DreamSync processes session data on the user's paired phone and watch. Remote does not operate a DreamSync server and does not receive health readings, session history, check-ins, or dream notes. DreamSync has no account system, ads, analytics, or automatic cloud upload.

## Phone and watch transfer

DreamSync transfers settings, cue requests, status, and session summaries between the user's paired Android phone and Wear OS watch through the Google Play services Wearable Data Layer. This transfer is required for core phone-watch operation.

## Export and deletion

Export creates a local file only after the user chooses a destination. Confirmed Delete removes DreamSync session history, cue events, check-ins, and received watch logs from the phone and queues a bound deletion command for the paired watch. Uninstalling the apps removes their local app data, subject to device settings and backups.

## Permissions

- Activity recognition and body-sensor or heart-rate permissions support cue timing and safety.
- Notifications and foreground services keep an active night session visible and provide a stop control.
- DreamSync does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or payment information.

## Intended use

DreamSync is an adult wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not measure EEG; diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition; or guarantee deeper sleep, memory, immune, or uninterrupted-sleep outcomes. Stop using DreamSync if sound disturbs sleep or causes adverse effects. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

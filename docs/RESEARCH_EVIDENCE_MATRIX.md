# DreamSync requirement and claim evidence matrix

Last verified: 19 July 2026

| Feature or public statement | Evidence basis | Implemented evidence | Claim limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dreams Redefined. | Brand slogan. | App onboarding, science screen, and store copy. | Slogan only; no outcome guarantee. |
| Designed to protect sleep continuity. | Product safety architecture; continuity is measured by awakenings, WASO, bout length, and disturbance. | Arousal backoff, cue shutdown, continuity tracker, morning disturbance rating, export. | Design goal, not proof that sleep improves. |
| Automatic likely-sleep detection while On. | Wear Health Services supports passive asleep-state updates on some devices and passive heart rate across compatible Wear OS devices. [Wear passive monitoring](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-services/monitor-background), [compatibility](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-services/compatibility) | Persistent On/Off; asleep-state path plus a time-windowed, three-sample passive-HR fallback; fresh automatic handshake; higher-rate sustained quiet plus fresh motion and HR must confirm likely sleep before any cue. | Passive batching is device-controlled. HR alone never authorizes a cue. Say likely or estimated; never confirmed clinical stage. |
| Seven-night personalization. | Multi-night within-person medians reduce reliance on one noisy night. This is an engineering choice requiring validation. | Room baseline records; latest seven valid nights; conservative defaults before seven. | Do not describe as clinically validated. |
| Samsung HR and IBI/HRV. | Samsung documents continuous HR with IBI. [SDK overview](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/overview.html), [data specifications](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/data-specifications.html) | Optional Samsung bridge plus standard Wear OS fallback; IBI median and RMSSD features. | Requires official AAR, compatible watch, permissions, package/signature approval, and live samples. |
| Samsung skin-temperature trend. | Samsung documents continuous skin temperature on supported Watch5-and-later hardware. [SDK introduction](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/introduction.html), [data specifications](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/data-specifications.html) | Temperature freshness, median, trend, logging, and baseline fields. | Trend is context, not core-body temperature or diagnosis. |
| Capability-selected raw PPG. | Samsung documents continuous green/red/IR PPG. [SDK data specifications](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/data-specifications.html) | The full-sensor profile requests PPG only when the approved provider reports it; unsupported watches stay on the core path. | Public use still requires battery, permission, partner approval, and live-device tests. |
| Off-wrist cue blocking. | Samsung HR status supports contact-quality/off-wrist handling. [Samsung SDK data specifications](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/data-specifications.html) | Off-wrist state enters signal quality and produces zero cue eligibility. | Only available when the Samsung stream reports the status. |
| Fully supported / Basic monitoring only / Not supported. | Capability-based compatibility avoids unsupported model-only claims. Wear OS documents required core and optional extra health capabilities; Samsung states Watch4+ support varies by tracker and model. [Wear compatibility](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-services/compatibility), [Samsung SDK introduction](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/introduction.html) | Manufacturer-neutral classifier, watch self-test, durable capability heartbeat, and phone compatibility screen. Full-sensor watches automatically enable all approved reported trackers. | Fully supported requires advanced signals and a passed live self-test; physical validation remains separate. |
| Partner and signing approval. | Samsung requires partner approval and app package/signature registration for distribution. [Partner process](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/process.html), [app verification](https://developer.samsung.com/health/sensor/guide/app-verification.html) | Conditional local AAR dependency and explicit setup instructions. | Standard sensor build works; advanced Samsung support cannot be claimed until approval is complete. |
| Paired 50 ms pink-noise cues, 1.075 s apart. | Acoustic pattern derived from laboratory slow-oscillation studies. [Besedovsky 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-02170-3), [Ngo 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.03.006) | Versioned preloaded waveform and timing tests. | DreamSync has no EEG and does not phase-lock. |
| Conservative cue budget and early-night window. | Slow-wave activity is stronger earlier in typical sleep; lab work does not establish a consumer open-loop dose. [Ngo 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.03.006), [Weigenand 2016](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.13334) | Default 25-145 min window, strict stability periods, at most 12 deep pairs, low gain ceiling. | Engineering safety policy; no effectiveness claim. |
| Stop after a cue-linked wake sign. | Avoiding sleep fragmentation takes priority over continued stimulation. | First cue-linked arousal lowers gain and disables cues; 20-minute pause for any arousal. | A wrist arousal is estimated, not EEG-confirmed. |
| Pre-sleep schedule, daylight, exercise, warm-bath, and breathing guidance. | CBT-I is first-line for chronic insomnia; passive heating has systematic-review support. [ACP guideline](https://doi.org/10.7326/M15-2175), [AASM guideline](https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.8986), [heating review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.smrv.2018.11.008) | In-app pre-sleep coaching screen. | General wellness education, not delivery of CBT-I or treatment. |
| Optional notification protection. | Reducing avoidable notifications supports the product continuity goal. | User-approved Android notification-policy access; priority mode only during an automatically detected session; previous state restored on summary, timeout, Off, or relaunch. | Alarms and priority contacts follow system settings; watch sync depends on user/device settings. |
| Learned watch-charge reminder. | Adequate watch battery is an operational prerequisite; reminder timing is a product convenience, not a clinical intervention. | After three completed records, local median sleep-start time schedules an inexact phone reminder three hours earlier. | The reminder estimates routine timing only and does not predict sleep. |
| Apnea concern referral. | Persistent gasping, choking, and fragmentation warrant clinical evaluation. | Coaching and safety copy directs users to qualified care. | DreamSync does not screen, diagnose, or treat sleep apnea. |
| Local-first data and user export. | Wear Data Layer supports paired-device state and assets; health data should be minimized. [Wear data client types](https://developer.android.com/training/wearables/data/client-types) | Room on both devices, session-bound protocol, local JSON/CSV export, deletion. | No autonomous cloud upload or AI access to raw identifiers. |
| One valid cue request plays at most once. | Safety invariant for transient device messaging. | Message IDs, session/device/version/expiry validation, lifecycle ACKs, allowance consumed at audio start, replay/property tests. | Must still be verified on attached hardware under reconnect and process-death tests. |
| Apple Watch planned. | Platform-neutral decisions can transfer, but watchOS background behavior differs. | Roadmap only. | Apple Watch is not supported in this release. |

## Evidence status labels

- **Implemented:** code and local automated evidence exist.
- **Hardware pending:** requires the named S23+ and Galaxy Watch device.
- **Study pending:** requires controlled multi-night or PSG validation.
- **External approval pending:** requires Samsung or Google approval outside the repository.

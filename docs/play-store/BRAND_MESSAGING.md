# DreamSync Official Brand Messaging

## Slogan

Dreams Redefined.

## Mission

DreamSync's mission is to protect sleep continuity by automatically estimating likely sleep from wearable signals, staying quiet when confidence is low, and using gentle, research-shaped cues only when strict safety checks indicate a low risk of disturbance.

## Short public message

Automatic sleep estimation with sleep-first safety.

## Watch compatibility

First validation targets Galaxy Watch Ultra 2024 and 2025. Galaxy Watch8, Watch8 Classic, and Watch7 are next. Watch4, Watch5, and Watch6 receive basic monitoring unless DreamSync's live motion, heart-rate, Samsung-signal, permission, and software checks pass. The app reports Fully supported, Basic monitoring only, or Not supported. Apple Watch, Pixel Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Galaxy Fit, and older Samsung Tizen watches are not supported in this release.

## Approved claim language

- Estimates likely sleep and conservative cue-ready windows.
- Designed to protect sleep continuity.
- Stays quiet when required safety checks are uncertain.
- Uses a research-shaped paired pink-noise pattern.
- Data stays on paired devices unless the user exports it.
- Shows estimated awakenings, wake after sleep onset, longest uninterrupted bout, cue-linked arousal rate, and morning disturbance rating.

## Do not claim

- Guaranteed uninterrupted sleep or prevention of waking.
- Proven deeper sleep, sleep-stage detection, or EEG phase locking.
- Improved memory, immunity, hormones, or treatment of a condition.
- Guaranteed lucid dreams.
- Physical validation on a watch model until that model has passed documented hardware tests.

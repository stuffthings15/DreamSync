## Full description

DreamSync pairs your Android phone with a Wear OS watch to deliver quiet, research-shaped sleep cues during conservative cue-ready windows. Protecting your sleep always takes priority over delivering a cue.

HOW IT WORKS

Tap Arm for tonight once. DreamSync sends fresh settings, opens the watch, and—after any first-use permission prompts—arms visible overnight sensing. Your watch then checks sustained low motion, fresh heart-rate data, and signal quality to estimate likely sleep onset automatically. Cue timing starts from that estimate, not from the button tap. If heart data is not reliable, DreamSync logs only and sends no cues. If sleep is not estimated within three hours, the session ends without sending cues.

DreamSync waits for a stable, low-motion period from 25 to 145 minutes after estimated sleep onset. A deep cue is two 50 millisecond pink-noise bursts, 1.075 seconds apart, with gentle 5 millisecond fades. Default deep mode requests no more than 12 paired cues. Any arousal sign starts a 20-minute quiet period. The first cue-linked wake sign stops further cues for the night.

SCIENCE, CLEARLY STATED

The acoustic pattern comes from peer-reviewed auditory sleep research. In a randomized crossover laboratory study of 14 healthy young men, Besedovsky and colleagues used EEG to phase-lock paired 50 dB pink-noise bursts to slow oscillations during non-REM sleep. About 172 paired stimulations were delivered on average across 120 minutes. The study reported increased slow-oscillation power and amplitude without changing time spent in measured sleep stages. Hormone analyses used 10 to 14 participants; immune-cell results used a nine-person subset; growth hormone was unchanged. DreamSync does not measure or claim those biological outcomes.

Earlier closed-loop studies by Ngo and colleagues reported effects on slow oscillations and memory under EEG control. A later open-loop study by Weigenand and colleagues did not improve word-pair memory, altered other sleep measures, and was noticed by half of its participants. DreamSync therefore makes no memory claim and uses low cue volume, conservative gating, long quiet periods, and immediate stopping after a cue-linked wake sign.

IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE

DreamSync does not measure EEG and cannot reproduce laboratory brain-wave phase locking. The study used in-ear headphones and calibrated 50 dB SPL; DreamSync blocks headphone routes and uses a user-calibrated phone speaker level, not a laboratory SPL value. Wrist motion and heart rate can only estimate conservative cue-ready windows; they do not diagnose a sleep stage. Lucid cues are a separate optional conditioning feature, and the cited studies do not prove that DreamSync causes lucid dreams.

Your session history, morning check-ins, and optional dream notes stay on your paired devices unless you choose Export. DreamSync has no accounts, ads, analytics, or automatic cloud upload. You can export or delete your local data at any time.

DreamSync is intended for adults age 18 and older. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not guarantee deeper sleep, memory improvement, immune effects, or lucid dreams. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Requires an Android phone and a compatible Wear OS watch with motion and heart-rate sensors. Reliable heart-rate access is required for automatic cueing.
